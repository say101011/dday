Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Welcome to d-day ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of your information when you use the d-day mobile application (the "App").

1. Data Collection & Local Storage

This app (d-day) securely stores all your schedule and anniversary data locally on your device only. We do not collect or transmit this data to the developer or any external servers.

All user-generated content, including but not limited to your custom D-Day event titles, target dates, selected categories, notes, and preferences, is saved locally on your device. We do not transmit, upload, or sync this information to any external servers. Because your data remains on your device, clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App will permanently delete your saved events.

2. Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

To provide this app for free, we use Google AdMob. Device advertising identifiers (AD_ID) and cookies may be collected to provide personalized ads and analytics.

In order to serve relevant advertisements, Google AdMob and its partners may automatically collect certain device information, which may include:

  • Advertising ID: A unique, user-resettable ID for advertising provided by your mobile operating system.
  • IP Address & Device Information: Network information and device type to help target ads appropriately.
  • Crash Logs & Analytics: Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports to monitor the health and performance of the ad network.

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK, we utilize the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to obtain your consent for personalized advertising and the use of cookies/local storage. You can manage or revoke your consent at any time within the App.

For more details on how Google uses this information, please review the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Notification Permissions

We use local notifications to remind you of upcoming D-Days and anniversaries.

The App requests notification permissions solely to provide you with timely reminders about your saved events. These notifications are generated locally on your device based on your stored data and do not rely on push notifications from external servers.

3. Children's Privacy

Our App does not target and is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information (e.g., through an error report or email communication), we will delete such information from our records immediately. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we will be able to take the necessary actions.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.
