Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Welcome to d-day ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of your information when you use the d-day mobile application (the "App").

1. Schedule Data, Local Storage, and Deletion

D-day processes your event titles, target dates, categories, notes, icons, sound preferences and pinned-event choices on your device. The app does not send this schedule content to a developer server or include it in advertising requests. No account registration or developer-operated schedule synchronization service is provided.

Events are kept in the app's internal database until you delete them. Individual events can be deleted in their details. Clearing app data in Android settings or uninstalling the app removes the app's current local database and preferences. These actions do not erase screenshots, information you copied elsewhere, or backups made by an older app version or other tools.

The current version disables Android app cloud backup and explicitly excludes its app files, databases and preferences from Android backup and device-transfer rules. It does not provide a schedule export or restore service. Reinstalling the app or changing devices may therefore result in loss of saved events. Device or third-party tools operating outside these app rules are governed by their own settings.

2. Google Advertising Services and Privacy Choices

The app includes Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Google SDKs may use network connections even though your schedule content is processed locally. Development builds use Google test advertising IDs; test advertising does not mean all SDK data processing is disabled.

Depending on the SDK configuration, device settings, region and privacy choices, Google Mobile Ads collects and shares data for advertising or marketing, analytics, and fraud prevention, security and compliance, including:

  • IP address: network information that may be used to estimate approximate location. The app does not request GPS or other Android location permissions.
  • App interactions: information such as app launches, taps and advertising interactions.
  • Diagnostic and performance information: information such as launch time, hangs and SDK or app performance. We do not describe these metrics as necessarily anonymous.
  • Device or other identifiers: Android advertising ID, app set ID and other identifiers as applicable to the SDK configuration. Advertising ID use can be affected by your device controls, consent and age-related advertising settings.

Google Mobile Ads encrypts the collected data in transit using TLS. The app does not provide a developer-operated repository of this advertising data. Google's retention, deletion and privacy controls are explained in the Google Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

UMP requests updated privacy information and displays the applicable published messages when required. Google Mobile Ads initializes and requests advertisements only when UMP permits ad requests. Applicable European messages cover the EEA, the UK and Switzerland; UMP also supports messages for applicable US state regulations. Regional message availability depends on the publisher's AdMob configuration. Where UMP requires a privacy-options entry point, the app displays Ad privacy options / 광고 개인정보 옵션 at the bottom of its screen to revisit your choices. This option is not shown in every region or consent state.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID using Android's advertising controls. You can use the core schedule features without agreeing to personalized advertising, and advertising failures do not block your locally stored events. Core schedule features work without network access; advertisements, online policies and consent forms may need connectivity.

3. Notification Permissions

We use local notifications to remind you of upcoming D-Days and anniversaries.

The App requests notification permissions solely to provide you with timely reminders about your saved events. These notifications are generated locally on your device based on your stored data and do not rely on push notifications from external servers.

4. Audience and Children's Privacy

D-day is intended for users aged 13 and older, and does not target children under 13. A neutral first-run age screen has no preselected answer and does not offer different schedule features to eligible profiles based on age. We store only an adult, protected or restricted status in this device's preferences, not your date of birth or exact age. Selecting under 13 restricts access to the app and does not start UMP, Google Mobile Ads or notification scheduling from the app. The selected status itself is not sent as an age range or birth date; Google services receive the appropriate age-protection signals when used.

Users aged 13–17 and users who prefer not to disclose their age receive the protected profile. UMP is told that these users are under the age of consent, and Google Mobile Ads is configured with CHILD age treatment as a conservative protection. This treatment restricts personalized advertising and remarketing and suppresses Android advertising ID transmission. All ad requests, including adult requests, have a maximum G content rating. Privacy Sandbox advertising permissions are removed. Protected advertising does not eliminate all SDK technical or network processing described above. Only an explicit 18-or-older selection uses the adult profile, with advertising subject to UMP and device privacy settings.

On a shared device, choose Prefer not to say to use the protected profile. The status is retained for future app launches. Clearing app data in Android settings removes this status and shows the age screen again, but also removes saved local events. If you believe an under-13 child has sent personal information to the operator through an inquiry, contact the email below so that we can review the request and delete information as appropriate.
